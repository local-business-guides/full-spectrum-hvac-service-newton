Preparing for a repair visit

A little preparation makes it easier to explain the problem. Before the technician arrives, consider writing down:

  • When you first noticed the trouble
  • What the system does when you try to use it
  • Any sounds or changes you’ve observed
  • Any steps you have already taken

Keep the area around the equipment clear if you’re able. Don’t take apart the system to look for a fault. If the symptoms come and go, note when you’ve noticed them. These details can help the technician investigate without asking you to guess at the cause.
